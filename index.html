What this tool can and cannot do

Encoding is not the same as encryption. Base64, hexadecimal and URL encoding are reversible formats, not secret-key encryption. Caesar, ROT13 and Atbash are classical ciphers. Vigenère and XOR need a key in this version.

This is not a universal breaker. Strong modern encryption (for example, properly used AES or RSA) cannot be recovered just by trying text transformations; you need the appropriate key and parameters. Hashes are one-way digests, not encrypted text.
